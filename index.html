How long does delivery take across the UAE?
Standard express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and major UAE cities. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM in Dubai.
What is Brisk's return and refund policy?
We offer a 14-day hassle-free return and full refund policy for unused items in original packaging. Renewed electronics come with a standard 12-month Brisk Warranty.
What does "Renewed" condition mean at Brisk?
Brisk Renewed products are pre-owned or open-box devices that have been professionally inspected, cleaned, tested, and restored to full original working condition.
What payment options are accepted?
We accept Visa, MasterCard, Apple Pay, Cash on Delivery (COD), and 0% interest monthly installment plans (Tabby / Tamara).
How can I track my existing order status?
Click on the ticket icon in the top header or visit our Track Order popup, enter your Order ID and Email to see live courier tracking updates.
